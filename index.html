Meet Angela

Two meanings.
One philosophy.

BAE. stands for By Angela Ewart, the person behind the treatments, and Before Anyone Else, the philosophy behind them.

It began with a personal journey through the changes of perimenopause, a lymphatic massage on a trip to Turkey, and a wish to create a place where caring for yourself feels normal, not indulgent.
