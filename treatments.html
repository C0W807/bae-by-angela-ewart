01

Lymphatic massage

  • DurationTBC mins
  • Price£ TBC
  • PressureVery light

Gentle, rhythmic massage techniques focused on the movement of lymphatic fluid.

Lymphatic massage is light, slow and repetitive. Rather than working deep into the muscle, Angela uses soft, sweeping strokes that follow the body's lymphatic pathways. Many people find it deeply calming.

What to expect

A short consultation first, then a quiet, unhurried treatment on the couch, draped in warm linen throughout. Wear something comfortable, and plan a gentle rest of the day if you can.

Is it right for me?

How it suits you depends on you as an individual. Some medical conditions need specialist advice or clearance from your GP before booking, and Angela will go through this with you in your consultation.

This treatment is for relaxation and wellbeing. It isn't a medical treatment and isn't a substitute for advice from your GP or a specialist.

02

Wood therapy

  • DurationTBC mins
  • Price£ TBC
  • PressureMedium to firm

A bodywork treatment using shaped wooden tools.

Hand-turned wooden rollers, cups and paddles are glided, rolled and pressed over the body with oil, each shape bringing a different rhythm and pressure. It's a firmer, more textured treatment than a classic massage, and many people find it grounding.

What to expect

After your consultation, Angela will work over the areas you've agreed together, checking the pressure with you as she goes. You'll feel the shape of the tools and their warmth from the hands that use them.

Is it right for me?

Wood therapy isn't suitable for everyone, so it's always discussed in your consultation first. You'll be asked about your health, any skin conditions and any recent treatments or surgery.

Wood therapy is a bodywork and relaxation treatment. It doesn't promise fat loss, detoxification or permanent changes to body shape.

Swedish massage

  • DurationTBC mins
  • Price£ TBC
  • PressureTo suit you

Classic flowing strokes and kneading to help you relax and ease everyday muscular tension.

The massage most of us know. Long, flowing strokes, kneading and gentle movements across the back, shoulders, arms and legs, with the pressure set to what feels good for you.

What to expect

A short chat about where you hold tension and how firm you like it, then time to switch off. You'll stay covered with warm linen except for the area being worked on.

Is it right for me?

Swedish massage suits most people. If you're pregnant, have a medical condition or have had recent surgery, mention it when you get in touch and Angela will advise.

Good to know

Questions

Which areas do you cover?

Dunfermline and the surrounding areas. If you're not sure whether you're close enough, just ask. Travel radius and any travel supplement: to confirm.

What do I need to have ready at home?

A little floor space, roughly the size of a single bed with room to walk around it, somewhere warm, and ideally a quiet room. Angela brings the couch, linen, towels and oils.

Where will Angela park?

Angela carries a couch, linen and towels, so she needs to park close to your door. If there are parking restrictions or permits where you live, mention it when you book.

When will Angela arrive?

A little before your appointment time, so she can set up quietly while you get comfortable. Setting up takes about TBC minutes.

Is everything fresh and clean?

Yes. Every appointment has its own freshly laundered linen and towels, and the couch and tools are cleaned between clients.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable. You'll undress to your level of comfort and stay covered with warm linen throughout, apart from the area being treated.

Which treatment should I choose?

If you want to switch off, Swedish massage is a lovely place to start. If you're drawn to something gentler and slower, try lymphatic massage. Wood therapy is firmer and more textured. Not sure? Tell Angela what you're looking for and she'll help you choose.

Can I book if I'm pregnant or have a medical condition?

Please mention it when you get in touch. Some treatments aren't suitable during pregnancy or with certain conditions, and some may need clearance from your GP first. Angela will always go through this with you before your appointment.
