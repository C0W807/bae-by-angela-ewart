Who we are

BAE. by Angela Ewart is a mobile massage service run by Angela Ewart, who is responsible for your information. You can contact her at email to confirm. ICO registration number: to confirm.

What we collect

  • When you enquire: your name, email, phone number if you give it, your town or area, the treatment you're interested in and your message.
  • Before your first treatment: a consultation form with your contact details and relevant health information, such as medical history, medications, allergies, pregnancy, recent surgery and skin conditions, so your treatment is safe and suitable for you. Health information is only collected with your explicit consent.
  • For appointments: the address you'd like Angela to visit, and any notes on parking or access.

How it's used

Only to reply to you, arrange and carry out your treatments, keep the records a therapist is expected to keep, and, if you ask, let you know about new treatments or offers. Your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

How it's kept

Consultation forms and health information are stored securely and seen only by Angela. Records are kept for period to confirm after your last appointment, in line with professional and insurance requirements, and then securely deleted.

Services this website uses

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to those services to load the page. Enquiries sent through the form are handled by form service to confirm.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information held about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Just get in touch. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
