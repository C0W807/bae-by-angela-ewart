Before Anyone Else

The philosophy behind them.

BAE. began with a personal journey.

As I've grown older and navigated the changes of perimenopause, I've become more curious about the human body, how it changes and how important it is to look after ourselves. That curiosity led to years of reading, researching and exploring approaches to health and wellbeing.

During a trip to Turkey, I experienced lymphatic massage for myself. I was struck by the difference I personally felt afterwards, and it inspired me to learn more about massage, the lymphatic system and the everyday habits that can help us feel well.

The more I explored, the more I wanted to create something meaningful: a place where caring for yourself feels normal, not indulgent.

And so BAE. was born.

BAE. stands for By Angela Ewart, the person behind the treatments, and Before Anyone Else, the philosophy behind them. It's a reminder to listen to your body, value rest, make time for movement and build small, realistic self-care habits.

Launching with lymphatic massage, wood therapy and Swedish massage, BAE. BODY offers thoughtful, individual treatments in the comfort of your own home across Dunfermline and surrounding areas.

My hope is that BAE. becomes more than an appointment. A place to learn, reconnect and take a little time for yourself, wherever you are in life.
